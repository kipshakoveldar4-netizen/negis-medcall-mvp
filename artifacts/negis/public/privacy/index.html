Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Medina OS is a business CRM and automation platform, including clinic, salon and mortgage team workflows. This Privacy Policy describes information processed through the CRM, advertising modules and connected integrations.

Information We Collect

Depending on the modules used, information may include organization names, cities, services and settings; employee names, emails, roles, permissions and work actions; contacts of customers, patients and mortgage applicants entered in the CRM or received through connected channels; inquiries, mortgage applications, deals, calls, appointments, tasks, messages and reports; advertising settings, budgets, audiences, results and Meta/Facebook/Instagram identifiers; creative images, videos, copy and prompts; and technical request, browser, error and security information.

If the direct website inquiry form is enabled, it submits a name, business name, phone or other contact offered by the form, selected services, page path and consent version. A server record of the inquiry and consent is stored to handle the inquiry and provide a callback. Form consent relates to that purpose, not every CRM or integration operation; this notice does not establish that existing customers have already consented.

How We Use Information

We use information to operate the CRM, handle leads and enabled direct website inquiries, provide callbacks, manage customers, appointments, tasks and communications, prepare advertising and reports, support connected integrations, diagnose errors and protect accounts.

Meta and Advertising Integrations

If an organization connects Meta/Facebook/Instagram Ads, Medina OS may use account, page, campaign, ad set, ad and creative identifiers to create, check, stop or analyze advertising. Advertising budgets are paid through the customer's Meta account and are not included in Medina OS fees.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Depending on the selected function, external providers may process data: Supabase for data and file storage, Meta for advertising campaigns and OpenAI for text generation or analysis.

If Wazzup incoming messages are connected, the CRM may receive message content, sender contacts and technical channel identifiers. Telegram is optional: when its integration is enabled, selected notification or material content may be transmitted to Telegram.

If Cloudflare Turnstile form protection is enabled, it can load and process technical request data before the form consent checkbox is selected. The checkbox concerns submission of the contact inquiry, not blanket consent to processing by every provider.

Actual processing arrangements with external providers and data storage and transfer locations require verification for the selected deployment and connected services. This page does not establish Kazakhstan hosting or compliance with Kazakhstan law.

Website Visit Statistics

Medina OS uses Vercel Web Analytics on medinaos.com to measure visits to known application sections. Page addresses are stripped of query parameters and fragments. Names, phone numbers, CRM content, customer IDs and staff IDs are not sent by this integration. Views with unsafe referrer addresses, identity attribution or feature-flag metadata are discarded. Visit statistics do not measure employee productivity. The provider processes technical request information needed for aggregate visit statistics. Analytics is not enabled by this integration on test deployments or the previous Vercel hostname.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect data from unauthorized access, alteration or deletion. Workspace access is limited by staff roles and organization settings.

Server-side integration secrets, service role keys, server-side integration tokens and app secrets are handled on the server, not displayed to ordinary users. The interface may show technical status or public account identifiers. User authentication sessions are separately handled by the browser.

Browser Storage and Retention

Browser localStorage and sessionStorage hold necessary authentication data, workspace selection, interface preferences and, in some modules, drafts. This storage is separate from visit statistics.

Data scope and retention depend on the processing purpose, function and applicable law; grounds for further retention require review when handling a request. CRM and AI content do not make bank decisions, approve mortgages or replace medical diagnosis.

Data Deletion

Users or authorized organization representatives may request deletion of their own data, or withdrawal of consent where processing relies on it, through the contact below. See the scope and verification instructions on our Data Deletion page.
