Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 24, 2026

A request to delete your own data from Medina OS, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, may be sent to: kipshakoveldar4@gmail.com

What to Include in Your Request

Include only minimal information needed to locate your record and reply:

  • The name or contact already used in the service and a reply contact
  • The organization or workspace, if known
  • Which of your data and request should be reviewed, and the inquiry source if known

Do not send identity documents, passwords, login codes or access tokens. Request ownership and authority checks should be limited to the information necessary for that purpose.

Request Review

Requests are reviewed with verification of ownership and the requester's authority. Applicable statutory deadlines govern request handling and any required action. The lawful scope of action is determined after reviewing the processing purpose, applicable law and grounds for further retention. This does not promise automatic deletion of all related data.

In-CRM Site-Inquiry Tool

The internal CRM tool, when enabled for the site and available to an authorized owner or administrator, is limited to one selected website inquiry and its consent copies. Linked work records may block deletion pending separate review. The tool does not replace email request handling.

The tool does not clear other inquiries, external providers or backups; those require separate review. A request does not authorize erasure of other clients or workspaces.

Meta / Facebook Data

If your data came through Facebook or Instagram Lead Ads, mention that source if known. The lawful scope of deletion is determined after reviewing request ownership, your rights and retention grounds. A request does not mean deletion of all account advertising data or other clients' data; data held by Meta requires separate consideration.
